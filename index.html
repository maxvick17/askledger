How it works

AskLedger reads your question with a small natural-language engine that runs entirely in your browser — no accounts, no uploads, no data ever leaves your device. It works out what you are asking (break-even, runway, markup, margin), picks out the numbers and what each one means, runs the exact textbook formula, and then explains the answer in plain English with every step shown, so you can check the maths yourself.

AskLedger is an educational calculator, not financial advice. Figures are estimates based on the numbers you provide.
